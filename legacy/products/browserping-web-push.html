BrowserPing · Module 1

Web Push Notifications

A native browser push channel wired directly into Customer Insights Journeys. Design the notification in the journey canvas like any other step — BrowserPing handles delivery to the browser.

Talk to Us

Native Custom Channel

Shows up as its own channel step in Customer Insights Journeys, alongside email and SMS — no separate tool to log into.

Reaches People Off-Site

Once someone subscribes, you can reach them even after they've closed the tab — no need for them to be on your site.

Delivery Reporting

Delivered / not-delivered status flows back into the journey, the same way it does for any other CI-Journeys channel.

Trigger & Segment-Based

Send from the same segments and triggers you already use for email and SMS journeys — no duplicate audience setup.

No App Required

Runs on standard browser push APIs — visitors subscribe with one click, no app store, no install.

Works Alongside Other Channels

Combine push with email, SMS, and — once enabled — in-page banners, all from the same journey.
